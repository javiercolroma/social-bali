# Landing (una página)

URL actual: **https://balicircle.vercel.app** (Vercel, proyecto `balicircle` del equipo Bali Circle, publica sola desde la carpeta `balicircle/` del repo javiercolroma/forgeloop-legal en cada push a main). Dominio propio pendiente: balicircle.club estaba libre el 2026-10-26.
Una sola pantalla. Fondo marfil, titular en serifa, un botón. Nada más.

---

**Titular**
Find your crew in Bali.

**Subtítulo**
A private social club for people who surf, train, run and stretch. Every day, 15 people nearby who share your sports. Say hi, go do something.

**Botón principal**
Download on the App Store → [ENLACE DE CAMPAÑA landing]

**Tres puntos (debajo del botón)**
- **Nearby, not everywhere.** Rounded distance only. Nobody sees your exact location.
- **15 a day.** Picked by distance, sports and who just arrived. No endless feed.
- **Not a dating app.** You can be open to dates if you want. Most people are looking for a surf or gym buddy.
