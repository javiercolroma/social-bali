## Post largo (Facebook)

**I built an app to find people to surf and train with in Canggu. Looking for the first 50 people to try it.**

Quick background: I'm a developer, and the same thing kept coming up with people arriving in Bali: you land, you want to surf or hit the gym, and you don't know anyone yet. You end up scrolling WhatsApp groups with 800 people and no idea who's actually around.

So I built Bali Circle. Every day it shows you 15 people nearby who are into the same things (surf, gym, yoga, running), with rough distance (never your exact location), what they're up to today, and a chat to just say "surfing Batu Bolong at 7, anyone?"

It's a small private circle, not a dating app (you can say you're open to dates if you want, but most people are just looking for a crew).

It's free and it's on the App Store now: [ENLACE DE CAMPAÑA facebook]

What I'm asking: install it, set up your profile (takes 2 min), and tell me honestly what's confusing or missing. I read and answer every message. The first 50 people shape what this becomes.

iPhone only for now, sorry Android friends.

## Respuestas preparadas para comentarios habituales

**"Another dating app"** → Fair worry. It isn't: there's no swiping and no matching. You see who's around, you see what they're into, you talk. You can mark yourself open to dates, but the default is finding a crew.

**"Android?"** → Not yet. If this works on iPhone first, Android follows. Drop your email on the site and I'll tell you when.

**"Why not just use the WhatsApp groups?"** → You can, and many do. The difference is seeing who's actually nearby right now and what they're doing today, instead of 800 people and no context.

**"Is my location shared?"** → Nobody ever sees coordinates, only rounded distance (like "1.2 km"). Screenshots are blocked inside the app too.
